Volume: Q1 2026 $1.8B / 81 venture rounds + 26 accelerator; Q2 2026 $1.7B /
64 venture rounds + 5 accelerator.

Deal counts published: Q1 vertical business software 30, hardware/robotics 17.
Q2 vertical business software 15, climate & energy 11, hardware/robotics 8,
life sciences & biotech 8, fintech 6, healthtech 6. No other sector has one.

**Fastest growing Q1→Q2 2026:** AI models & data infrastructure +630%
($100M→$730M); healthtech +46%. Fintech rose from outside the top ten to
second. **Sharpest falls:** cybersecurity −86%, vertical business software
−84%, space & defence −72%, horizontal business software −67%.

Prior year: CY2025 $5.4B across 390 deals, +31% YoY, third-largest on record —
AI $1.0B, fintech $868M, biotech/medtech $829M (49 deals, the deal-count
leader). 61% of all capital went to startups building with AI in the stack.

**Divergence rule — CONSTRUCTED, NOT MEASURED.** Cut Through publishes medians,
not ranges, so these bounds are a stated choice (⅓× to 3× the Q2 2026 median),
not an observed distribution. Flag a round outside:

## Coverage limits — read these before flagging

1. **Q1's sector list is a top ten.** It sums to $1.63B of a stated $1.8B, so
   ~$170M sits in sectors it does not name. "Not in top 10" means *below $76M*,
   not zero. Q2's eleven entries sum to $1.71B against a stated $1.7B, so that
   quarter is effectively complete.
2. **The 2025 taxonomy is different** — "Artificial Intelligence",
   "Biotech/Medtech" — so only fintech survives the boundary unchanged. Do not
   compute growth across it.
3. **One quarter is one observation.** AI's +630% is a single quarter-on-quarter
   move on a base of $100M, and Q2's $730M is concentrated enough that one large
   round could dominate it. Treat it as the direction, not the rate.
4. Grants and government money (QIC Ventures, state programmes) are not venture
   rounds and are not in these figures.
